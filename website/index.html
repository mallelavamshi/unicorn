Fix it before you send it.

Type the way you text. Tap a tone and Unicorn rewrites it in place, in Messages, WhatsApp, Gmail, Instagram or anywhere else you type.

  • /reply answers the last message you got, in your voice.
  • /email turns a messy thought into a finished email.
  • /translate does Spanish, French and twenty more.

Private by design.

Your prompts stay on your phone.

Saved questions are never uploaded and aren't included in phone backups. Delete them all with one tap.

Private fields stay private.

In passwords, codes, card numbers and banking apps, Unicorn steps back: no AI, no saving, no learning. Just plain keys.

Locked to your fingerprint.

Turn on the app lock and Unicorn opens only with your fingerprint, face or your phone's screen lock.

Free, with nothing to sign up for.

All of Unicorn works without an account. Sharing trends is off until you turn it on, and you can see exactly what it sends first.
