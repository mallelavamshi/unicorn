# Unicorn launch video for X: storyboard

Status: built, 2026-11-04. The first full render is in `video/out/`. How to re-shoot and re-render is in `video/README.md`.

- **Format:** vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 50 s. X autoplays muted, so every point is on screen as text.
- **Music:** an upbeat electronic track of about 124 BPM, free and licensed for X (Mixkit or Pixabay), chosen by Claude. Cuts and zooms land on the beat; one beat is about 0.48 s.
- **Positioning:** "Unicorn. The social AI keyboard." The word "keyboard" is allowed in this X video only (user decision, 2026-11-04). The Play listing, the website and the app keep the rule.

## Look

- **Palette:** night violet #1C1035 behind everything, violet #7430A4, gold #F2C14E for sparkles, lilac #F3EEFA for light cards.
- **Type:** Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, the website's display font. Words slam in on the beat with a springy overshoot and a soft violet-to-pink glow. One key word per line gets a gold sparkle.
- **Zoom boxes:** a glowing rounded square snaps around a feature on the beat. The camera punches into it in about 0.2 s, holds, then punches out or cuts.
- **Transitions:**
  - zoom-through, which scales into the next shot with motion blur and a white flash, between shots;
  - a glitter wipe at each act change;
  - a whip-slide between the AI tools.
- **Phone:** the real screen with rounded corners and a soft glow. There's no branded device frame.

## Honesty rules

These rules still hold:

- Every phone shot is the real app. AI answers are real: about 0.7 s of the real spinner, then a cut (user decision).
- **The Act 2 prompt is real.** It's typed into the real Google app on the emulator, with made-up, demo-safe wording, and Unicorn really saves it. The emulator's fake ChatGPT screen is never used.
- **AI app icons (user decision, 2026-11-04).** The five apps' icons are animated in shot 5. They're trademarks, so they sit on a separate layer that can be swapped for name chips if any company objects.
- Hunches keep "Probably on …'s mind" and their reasons.
- Community trends only appear once 25 people share. Until then the video shows the opt-in card, never made-up trends.
- There's no Play Store page, because the listing isn't live. The journey starts at the home-screen icon.

The track is "Take this Higher" (Mixkit, 124 BPM). Every cut lands on a beat.

**Changes from the storyboard:**

- **Prompt saving, filmed for real.** The emulator has the real Google app, and Google is one of the five sources Unicorn saves from. The prompt is typed there with Unicorn's keys, Unicorn really saves it, and the Library and search shots show that saved entry.
  - Searching "vacation" finds it, although the word isn't in the prompt.
  - The 🕘 chip offers it again when "weekend in austin" is typed.
- **Live Google suggestions are kept off screen.** The Google shots start once the typed words make Google's suggestions harmless. Earlier letters showed trending news, real people and other brands ("plan a co…" suggested a contraceptive).
- **The share sheet is cut before it opens.** The emulator lists another session's test app, "FlorisBoard Debug", among the share targets. The card shot ends on the "Share my Unicorn" tap.
- **Learn my aim is in the caption only** ("It learns your aim."). It has nothing to show on screen, and scrolling its settings screen risked dragging the size sliders.
- **Dropped for length:** Insights and the emoji palette. Their takes are kept in `video/takes/`.
- **Hunches** are filmed on Near you, where each one shows its "Probably on …'s mind" label and its reason. The town comes from the emulator's Texas location (Prosper), and Austin when the neighborhood option is on.
- **Trends:** "What people near you ask AI" shows the real state, "1 of 25 people in Texas shared today", with no made-up trends.
