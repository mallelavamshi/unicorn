## How it works

Glide typing is decoded by `ai.unicorn.glide.GlideDecoder` (the AOSP native gesture decoder is an empty stub).
It is shape-and-location template matching after SHARK2 (Kristensson and Zhai, UIST 2004): the swipe and each candidate
word's ideal path over the real key centers are resampled and compared point by point, after scaling to a unit box,
and with an elastic alignment, plus a check that every key of the word was visited in order. Candidates come from
the words whose first and last keys are near where the swipe started and ended and whose path length fits. The
evidence is weighed with the word's frequency in the engine's English dictionary, the previous word (a next-word table
from Tatoeba's English sentences, CC BY 2.0 FR, plus what the user typed before), and the user's own words.

Synthetic swipes (`app/src/glidesynth`) follow a word's keys with the mistakes people make, in millimeters: realistic is
1.6 mm aim at each key (2 mm at the first and last), corners cut by up to 2.5 mm, 30% overshoot at sharp turns,
3 mm rounding, 0.25 mm sensor jitter, a wiggle on 35% of double letters and 0.7 to 1.5 times normal speed; sloppy is
2.4 mm aim, 4 mm cuts, 50% overshoot of up to 4 mm and up to 2.2 times the speed. A phone key is about 7 mm wide.

## Known limits

- Words whose keys lie on one straight line share a path ("for" and "four", "to" and "too", "its" and "it's").
  The previous word usually picks the right one; otherwise it is the first alternative in the suggestions.
- Synthetic swipes are not people. The emulator test injects them as real touches, but a real hand on a real phone
  is the final check.
- Two-letter words on neighboring keys ("we", "as", "ok") are quicker to tap. A swipe that short often isn't
  recognized as one before the key's long press opens its accents or number (the engine's gesture detection, as in
  AOSP), so the sentence test taps them, as people do.
- Short words that double back ("wow", "here" as a message's first word) can lose to a more common word with
  nearly the same path; it is then in the suggestions.
- English only, like the dictionary.
